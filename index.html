FAQs

Common questions

Can I change the itinerary?

Yes. Every package can be customised. Tell us the places you want and the number of days, and we will adjust the plan.

What is the best time to visit Uttarakhand?

March to June is good for hill stations and sightseeing. Winter (December to February) is best for snow at Auli and Chopta. Corbett safari zones have different opening seasons, so check your dates with us.

Where does the tour start?

Packages starting at Ramnagar begin near Jim Corbett. Others start from Nainital, Mussoorie or Rishikesh. We can also arrange pickup from the nearest railway station or Delhi.

What is included in the price?

Your quote lists cab, hotel stay and meals plan clearly. Entry tickets, safari and activities are shown separately so you can choose.

Is it suitable for families with kids and senior citizens?

Yes. We plan comfortable driving hours and suitable hotels. Tell us the ages of your group when you enquire.
